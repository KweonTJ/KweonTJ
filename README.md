## Research & systems

- **[Go2 locomotion](https://github.com/KweonTJ/HIMLoco_Horizontal)** — Learning stable quadruped motion over uneven terrain, with body-motion and path-tracking evaluation in Isaac Gym.
- **[OMX learning](https://github.com/KweonTJ/omx_train_ws) / [Robot runtime](https://github.com/KweonTJ/Omx_Turtle_Bot)** — PPO residual control for pick-and-place, linking MuJoCo training with ArUco perception and ROS 2.
- **[3D mobile manipulation](https://github.com/KweonTJ/3D_pereception_Based_Mobile_Manipulator-Autonomous_Navigation_System)** — RGB-D localization, coordinate transforms, manipulation, and TurtleBot3 leader–follower transport.
- **[AI Rescue Box](https://github.com/KweonTJ/AI_Rescue_Box)** — Jetson-based SLAM and person detection, floor-plan alignment, and UWB rescue-map messaging.
- **[MORAI driving](https://github.com/KweonTJ/MORAI_Simulation) / [CAN](https://github.com/KweonTJ/CAN_Protocol)** — Pure Pursuit, PID, LiDAR perception, and vehicle communication.
